## Platform

web

## Users
People in Brazil thinking about getting a tattoo, most of them first or second timers, browsing on a phone after seeing the studio on Instagram or TikTok. Their job: decide whether this studio's drawing is good enough for their skin, understand how a session works, and start a conversation to book. *(inferred)*

Secondary audience: recruiters and clients looking at the owner's portfolio. The site is a concept piece. *(confirmed: "o Pk'House é um teste")*

## Product Purpose
Single-page site for Pk'House Studio, a tattoo studio, that turns a visit into a WhatsApp conversation about a specific design. Success: the visitor sees the studio's hand, trusts the hygiene, and taps to book with an idea in mind.

## Positioning
The studio draws custom pieces with the client instead of selling stock designs. *(inferred from the incumbent copy: "Cada tatuagem é única, desenvolvida em colaboração com o cliente")*

## Operating Context
Booking happens on WhatsApp. Discovery happens on Instagram (@pkhousestudio) and TikTok. Visitors arrive on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stack: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS, lucide-react icons. Keep it.
- Deploy: GitHub Pages, as a static build.
- Styles offered by the incumbent gallery: realismo, tradicional, minimalista, geométrica, aquarela, blackwork, oriental.
- Hours in the incumbent footer: segunda a sexta, sábados, domingos (exact times not stated as facts).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Pk'House Studio. Tagline in use: "Arte na Pele".
- Portuguese (Brazil) copy.

## Evidence on Hand
None. There are no real photos of work, no real testimonials, no real numbers, and no real WhatsApp number. The incumbent's testimonials ("Maria Silva"…), stats ("1000+ tatuagens", "10+ anos") and placeholder images are invented and must not ship as fact. Any demonstration artwork is authored for the concept and labeled as such; the site must say it is a concept project with a fictional studio.

## Product Principles
1. Show the drawing, not the promise: the hand of the artist is the proof.
2. Booking starts with an idea: help the visitor arrive at WhatsApp knowing what they want.
3. Hygiene and aftercare are stated plainly, never as fear marketing.
4. Nothing invented reads as fact.
