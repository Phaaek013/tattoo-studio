# Surface: Pk'House Studio landing (src/App.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: people considering a tattoo, on a phone, arriving from Instagram/TikTok; secondary: portfolio viewers. Action: start a WhatsApp booking with a chosen style and idea (demo mode: no real number, the message is shown, not sent). Proof: authored woodcut demonstration prints, labeled as such; no invented testimonials, stats or prices. Constraint: concept project with a fictional studio, stated in the footer.

Direction chosen: candidate 3 of the grounded list (cordel woodcut stall), assigned by the roll; owner said to proceed without a decision round. Memorable moment: the varal of folhetos that is also the style selector.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio as a cordel stall at the feira: every style is a woodcut folheto pinned to a string, and picking one off the string is how you start your order. Refuses the black-page-with-orange-accent tattoo template and the photo grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cheap folheto papers as whole-section fields (gema yellow, chiclete pink, limão green, anil blue, and tangerina orange for the care section, a fifth cordel paper added during the build) with paper grain; one black ink, carved, rough-edged, tone by gouged lines and hatching only, colour never inside the ink except translucent wash on the aquarela print. Heavy slab woodtype display, sturdy book serif text. Barbante string, wooden pregadores.

STORY: The visitor sees that this studio draws, scans the styles as prints, learns how a custom piece is made (verse plus plain steps), reads hygiene and aftercare plainly, and sends a WhatsApp order pre-filled with style, idea, placement and size.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Gema paper. Left, "Arte na Pele" in woodtype at display max, one line of copy, primary button "Pedir orçamento" naming the selected style. Right, a large framed woodcut cover print (swallow carrying the studio banner). Across the bottom edge, the varal: seven folhetos on a string, selectable, swaying.

FORM: Cordel woodcut folheto stall; grounded list position 3 of 7; seed:bb5cba2ff1e600fa. Raises: from the HyperCard stack, one-ink discipline (no grey fills, tone only by cut line); from the fashion flagship, one monumental word per section with real negative space.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
